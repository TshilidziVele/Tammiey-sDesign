Our Design Process

  1. Initial Consultation: We meet with you to discuss your vision, preferences, and requirements.
  2. Design Concept: We create initial design sketches and discuss options with you.
  3. Fabric Selection: Together, we choose the perfect fabric that matches your vision and preferences.
  4. Measurements: Precise measurements are taken to ensure the perfect fit.
  5. Sample/Fitting: An initial fitting is scheduled to check the garment and make adjustments.
  6. Final Tailoring: We make final adjustments and complete the garment to perfection.
  7. Delivery: Your custom garment is delivered ready to wear.
